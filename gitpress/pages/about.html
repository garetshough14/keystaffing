Leadership Team

Founder & CEO

Jackie Koster

Jackie Koster founded Key Staffing & Consulting with a simple belief: workforce challenges are rarely just hiring problems. They are business problems that require the right people, strong leadership, and operational discipline to solve.

Over the past decade, she has built KSC into one of the Central Valley's trusted workforce partners by helping businesses strengthen their teams through staffing, recruiting, onsite workforce solutions, and long-term people strategies. Her approach extends far beyond filling positions. She focuses on helping organizations create stability, improve performance, and build teams that support sustainable growth.

As a business owner, leadership coach, and strategic advisor, Jackie works with organizations across multiple areas of business growth, including workforce strategy, leadership development, operational improvement, and executive support. She is passionate about developing leaders at every level and believes that investing in people creates stronger companies, healthier cultures, and better business outcomes.

Today, Jackie continues to lead KSC with a relationship-first philosophy while helping businesses build the structure, leadership, and workforce needed to scale with confidence. Her commitment to service, accountability, and continuous improvement remains the foundation of everything she and her team do.
